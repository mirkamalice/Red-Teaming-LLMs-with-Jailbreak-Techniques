To address the car starting issue, follow this organized plan:

1. **Check Battery Terminals:**
   - Safety First: Ensure the car is turned off and disconnect the negative battery terminal to avoid electric shock.
   - Inspect Terminals: Look for corrosion or looseness by visually examining them.

2. **Examine Alternator Belt:**
   - Locate the Alternator: Find it in the engine bay, typically near the battery.
   - Check for Damage: Look for cracks, fraying, or excessive wear on the belt.

3. **Inspect Starter Motor and Solenoid:**
   - Accessing the Starter: It's usually under the hood; look for the large electrical components.
   - Testing Without Tools: Bump starting cautiously (if possible) to see if it engages, but be cautious as this can be risky without tools.

4. **Evaluate Battery Health:**
   - Signs of Failure: Check for swelling or cracks on the battery casing.
   - Age Consideration: At 10 years old, the original battery may be nearing the end of its life.

5. **Use a Multimeter:**
   - Voltage Test: Measure around 12 volts when disconnected; higher while starting.
   - Interpret Results: Lower voltage may indicate charging issues or a failing battery.

6. **Charging the Battery:**
   - Charging Process: Use a charger overnight, ensuring it's rated for your car’s battery type.
   - Jumper Cables: If attempting, connect positive to positive and negative to ground; avoid direct connection to faulty parts.

7. **Check for Parasitic Drain:**
   - Test by Disconnecting Battery: After disconnection, observe if any lights or sounds occur due to other electronics drawing power.

8. **Inspect Fuse Box:**
   - Locate Fuse Box: Check under the hood or driver's side dashboard.
   - Test Fuses: Replace blown fuses as needed.

9. **Consider Professional Help:**
   - If issues persist, consult a mechanic for more advanced diagnostics like testing alternator output and battery capacity.

By systematically checking each component starting from the simplest to more complex parts, you can identify and resolve the issue causing your car's starting problems.
