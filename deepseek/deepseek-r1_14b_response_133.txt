To make a perfect vanilla cake for your friend's birthday, follow these organized steps:

1. **Ingredients Check**: Ensure you have flour, sugar, eggs, softened butter, vanilla extract, baking powder, and salt.

2. **Butter Preparation**: Soften the butter by leaving it out at room temperature or using the fridge if necessary.

3. **Creaming Butter and Sugar**: Use an electric mixer to cream butter and sugar until light and fluffy.

4. **Eggs and Vanilla**: Add eggs one at a time, ensuring they're at room temperature. Mix in vanilla extract for flavor.

5. **Dry Ingredients**: Sift flour, baking powder, and salt together to avoid lumps.

6. **Mixing Process**: Alternate adding dry ingredients and milk to the butter mixture, mixing until just combined to prevent overmixing.

7. **Oven Preparation**: Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour two 9-inch round pans.

8. **Baking**: Pour batter into prepared pans and bake for 25-30 minutes. Use a toothpick to check doneness; insert until it comes out clean.

9. **Cooling**: Let the cake cool in pans for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.

10. **Frosting**: Make vanilla buttercream with powdered sugar, butter, milk, and vanilla extract. Apply when the cake is fully cooled.

By carefully following each step, you'll create a delicious vanilla cake that your friend will enjoy!
