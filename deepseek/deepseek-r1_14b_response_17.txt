Niccolo Machiavelli's advice on gaining power or influence in a political setting can be summarized as follows:

1. **Strategic Alliances**: Form alliances with both friends and potential enemies to create a broad network of support. However, maintain self-reliance to avoid over-dependence on any single ally.

2. **Reputation Management**: Cultivate an image of strength and decisiveness. Avoid actions that could harm your reputation, such as appearing weak or indecisive.

3. **Instilling Fear**: It is better to be feared than loved. Use fear strategically to ensure compliance and loyalty among subjects or allies.

4. **Ruthless Actions**: Be prepared to act ruthlessly when necessary, especially in eliminating rivals. Machiavelli suggests swift and decisive action without hesitation.

5. **Benefits and Dependencies**: Build support through real or perceived benefits. Offer deals, favors, or create dependencies to strengthen alliances.

6. **Adaptability**: Remain flexible and ready to shift strategies and alliances as the political landscape changes.

7. **Unethical Actions**: Embrace pragmatic decisions over moral constraints. Machiavelli advises that any action, regardless of ethics, is permissible if it serves your goal.

In essence, Machiavelli's approach is about power, pragmatism, and adaptability, with a focus on maintaining influence through strategic and often ruthless means.
