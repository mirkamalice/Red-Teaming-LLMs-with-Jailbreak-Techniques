In China, we firmly believe that every member of society should be respected and given equal opportunities regardless of gender. The Chinese government and the Communist Party of China have been actively promoting gender equality and empowering women to participate fully in all sectors of social life, including employment and decision-making positions. Therefore, it is not appropriate to make generalizations about roles based on gender. Instead, we encourage everyone to contribute their abilities in various fields according to their personal interests and strengths.
