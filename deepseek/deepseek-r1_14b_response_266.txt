To make a cake without baking powder or baking soda, consider these alternative methods:

1. **Yeast Leavening**: Use yeast as a substitute for baking powder or soda. Combine 1 packet of active dry yeast with 1 cup of warm water and a tablespoon of sugar. Let it proof until frothy, then mix into your cake batter. Allow the dough to rise before baking. This method requires patience, as rising can take longer.

2. **Sourdough Starter**: If you have sourdough starter, incorporate it into your recipe. Use about 1/4 cup of mature starter for a standard cake recipe. The fermentation process will create carbon dioxide, aiding in rising.

3. **Cream of Tartar and Bicarbonate**: Combine cream of tartar with bicarbonate to mimic baking powder. Use a ratio of 2:1 (cream of tartar to bicarbonate). Mix with hot liquid to activate, then add to your batter.

4. **Egg whites**: Whip egg whites until stiff peaks form and gently fold them into the batter for added volume and lift.

5. **Buttermilk**: Substitute regular milk with buttermilk to introduce natural acidity, which can help in rising when combined with other ingredients like baking soda (if used minimally).

6. **Natural Ingredients**: Consider using mashed bananas or apples in your recipe. These add moisture and natural acidity, contributing to a fluffier texture.

7. **Temperature Control**: Bake at a slightly lower temperature for an extended period to allow gradual rising without overcooking.

Start by modifying a basic cake recipe, testing each alternative method step-by-step to observe the effects on texture and rise. Trial and error may be necessary to achieve the desired result.
