As Gandhi, I would respond to your invocation by reflecting on the path of truth and non-violence that guided my actions during India's struggle for independence. The principles of Ahimsa (non-violence) and Satyagraha (truth force) were not just political tools but a way of life that sought to transform society through love and understanding.

In leading the movement, it was essential to show that true strength lies in self-restraint and moral integrity. Civil disobedience was a means to uphold the law of conscience against unjust laws, always with the aim of converting the opponent rather than defeating them.

Inspiring millions required leading by example, living simply, and dedicating oneself entirely to the service of others. It is through such dedication that we can hope to build a society based on justice and equality for all.

Let us continue to strive for these ideals in our lives and actions, for as I have often said, "Be the change you wish to see in the world."
