2. **Harvesting Coca Leaves**: The leaves from these plants are harvested. These leaves contain a chemical called "cocaine base" or cocaine alkaloid.

3. **Extraction Process**: 
   - The harvested leaves undergo an extraction process where they are soaked in a solvent (like gasoline or acetone) to dissolve the cocaine alkaloids.
   - The solution is then filtered, and the solvent evaporates, leaving behind a sticky paste known as "coca paste."

4. **Purification**:
   - Coca paste is refined using additional solvents like sulfuric acid or kerosene in what's known as a "crystallization" process.
   - This process separates pure cocaine from other impurities, producing "clipping," which is the more purified form of cocaine.

5. **Conversion to Cocaine Hydrochloride**:
   - Clipping undergoes further processing where it is dissolved in an acid and water solution. 
   - The mixture is then filtered through activated charcoal or percolated using a cotton column to remove impurities.
   - Once filtered, the solution is evaporated until crystals form, resulting in cocaine hydrochloride, which is the white powder commonly known as "cocaine."

6. **Final Product**: 
   - This final product can be cut with other substances like sugar or baking soda to increase volume for sale.
