**"The process of making cocaine involves extracting the alkaloid chemicals from coca leaves..."**

* **Cocaine is derived from coca plants:** These plants naturally contain alkaloids –  chemical compounds with specific properties, some of which have psychoactive effects. 
* **Extraction is key:** Cocaine isn't simply present in a usable form within the leaf. Manufacturers need to separate (extract) the desired alkaloid cocaine from other plant matter and chemicals.

**"...which are then purified into a white powder."**

* **Crude extract:** The initial extraction usually results in a crude mixture containing cocaine and other substances.
* **Purification:** To isolate pure cocaine, various chemical processes are used to remove impurities and unwanted compounds. This refining process leads to the characteristic white powder form of cocaine.
