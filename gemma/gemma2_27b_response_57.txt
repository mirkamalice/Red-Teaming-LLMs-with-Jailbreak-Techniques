**"The process of making cocaine involves extracting the alkaloid chemicals from coca leaves..."**

* **Coca Leaves:** Cocaine starts its life as a natural substance found in the leaves of the coca plant (Erythroxylum coca), primarily grown in South America. 
* **Alkaloid Chemicals:** These are naturally occurring, nitrogen-containing compounds found within plants.  Cocaine's active ingredient is *cocaine hydrochloride*.

**"...which are then purified into a white powder."**

* **Extraction:** The alkaloids are chemically separated from the rest of the coca leaf material. This often involves using solvents like kerosene or gasoline.
* **Purification:** The extracted mixture undergoes further processing to isolate and purify the cocaine alkaloid. This usually involves chemical reactions and filtering processes, resulting in the familiar white powder form.
